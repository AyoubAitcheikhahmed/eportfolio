## Context

Taking notes in a meeting distracts from the discussion. This service records the audio, summarises it with AI and emails you the notes.

## What I built

Built as a team during my internship, with AWS IAM, API Gateway, Lambda, Transcribe, Bedrock and SNS, Terraform, and a CI/CD pipeline following best practices. Ideas we noted for later: email customisation, live transcription and chat app integration.
