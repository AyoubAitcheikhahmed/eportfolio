## Context

Restaurants handled bookings manually, which led to errors, double bookings and no visibility.

## What I built

A reservation system from scratch with Node.js, Express, MongoDB and React: APIs for reservations, tables, zones, users, login, token refresh and feedback, admin dashboards and custom planning tables. Hosted on AWS, with SES emails to inform guests about their reservation.

Restaurants gained full control over reservations, double bookings dropped, and real-time dashboards and automated emails improved satisfaction and staff efficiency.
