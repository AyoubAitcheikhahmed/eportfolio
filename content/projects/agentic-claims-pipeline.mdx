## Context

For a client, healthcare reimbursement files were processed mostly by hand from scanned documents. That was slow and error-prone when classifying documents and extracting data.

## What I built

An agentic AI pipeline with three agents: a **Classifier**, an **Extractor** and a **Suggester**. Together they classify each file, extract the relevant data and generate a reimbursement proposal. A human still validates the critical decisions.

- LangChain4j on Quarkus, MCP for tool orchestration, Claude models through AWS Bedrock.
- Test datasets: 60 scenarios across 20 care domains.
- A reporting tool with confidence-based colour coding to validate results.
